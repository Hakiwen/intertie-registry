Intertie implementations

These chat tools and adapters implement Intertie, the open protocol that lets two organizations share a channel directly. Each entry links the signed conformance report its maker submitted.

This is a directory, not a service. Messages between organizations never pass through this site or through its operator. Every listing is self-certified: the implementer ran the conformance suite against their own endpoint and signed the results with the key published at https://<their-org>/.well-known/intertie. CI checks that signature and the report's schema on every submission. A listing does not mean anyone audited the implementation.
